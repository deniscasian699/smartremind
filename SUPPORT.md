## 🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

### ⏰ Reminders Arriving Late

- Allow notifications and, where Android requests it, Alarms & reminders access.
- Check the reminder time, repeat schedule and clock format.
- Review battery restrictions and Do Not Disturb settings.
- Android and manufacturer restrictions can still delay delivery.

### 📱 Widgets Not Refreshing

Some Android manufacturers restrict background app activity.

1. Go to **Android Settings → Battery**
2. Find **SmartRemind**
3. Set battery optimization to **Unrestricted** or **No restrictions**

Affected manufacturers include Xiaomi (MIUI), Huawei (EMUI), Samsung
(One UI), and OnePlus (OxygenOS).

### 🔔 Notifications Not Arriving

1. Ensure notification permission is granted:
   **Android Settings → Apps → SmartRemind → Notifications → Allow**
2. Check that **Do Not Disturb** mode is not blocking the app.
3. Disable battery optimization for SmartRemind (see above).
4. Verify the reminder or habit schedule is active in SmartRemind.

### 💎 Premium Not Activating

1. Ensure you are signed in to Google Play with the account used for purchase.
2. Try: **SmartRemind → Support → Restore Purchases**
3. Wait a few minutes and restart the app.
4. If still not working, contact us with your **Google Play Order ID.**

### ❤️ Monthly Subscription Still Active After Buying Lifetime

Google Play does not allow apps to cancel subscriptions on your behalf.
You must cancel the monthly subscription manually:

→ [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)

Your Lifetime access is already active. Cancelling the subscription
simply stops future monthly charges.

### 🔔 How to Remove Ads

Purchase either the **Supporter** (one-time) or **Monthly Supporter**
(subscription) tier. After purchase, all advertising is removed permanently
for Supporter, or for the duration of your subscription for Monthly Supporter.

**Note:** Premium and Premium Lifetime do **not** remove advertising.
They unlock features such as unlimited reminders and habits, custom sounds,
individual colors, priorities and reports. The one-time ad-removal product
may be displayed as **Remove Ads** or **No Ads** in the app.

Ads may return after an ad-removal purchase is refunded or revoked, or when
Monthly Supporter expires. Use Restore Purchases with the purchasing Google
Play account if an active entitlement is not recognized.

### 🛡️ Advertising Privacy Choices

Open **Settings → Ad Privacy Choices** when available to revisit the
Google UMP privacy form. The entry appears when required for your region
and is hidden when advertising is disabled by an eligible purchase.
Your reminder and habit content is not sent to advertisers.

## 💳 Billing & Refunds

All billing is managed by **Google Play.** For refund requests:

1. Go to [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)
2. Find your SmartRemind purchase.
3. Follow Google Play's current refund procedure; eligibility depends on
   the purchase and applicable refund rules.

For assistance, contact us at
[support@rdcapps.com](mailto:support@rdcapps.com) with your
Google Play Order ID and we will do our best to help.
