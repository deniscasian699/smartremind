## Common Questions

### A reminder did not fire

Allow notifications and exact alarms, remove restrictive battery optimization, and avoid force-stopping the app.

### The clock icon is missing

Review exact-alarm permission and recreate or resave the reminder.

### A widget is not updating

Resize or re-add the widget, then check battery restrictions.

### Premium did not restore

Use Restore Purchases with the original Google account and an active connection.

## Billing and Refunds

Billing is handled by Google Play. Manage subscriptions at [Google Play Subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Refund requests are governed by [Google Play’s refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

Never post payment details, full order receipts, private QR content, reminder text, travel notes or other sensitive information in a public issue.
