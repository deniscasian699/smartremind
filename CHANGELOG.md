# Changelog

Notable changes to SmartRemind are documented here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/).

---

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Google AdMob adaptive banners, native ads, interstitials and app-open ads
- Google UMP consent and applicable advertising privacy choices
- Ad-removal support for the one-time Remove Ads / Supporter product and active Monthly Supporter subscriptions
- Reserved banner and native-ad space with loading and support messages
- Updated privacy, terms and app documentation for advertising

### Changed

- Premium Monthly and Premium Lifetime unlock features without removing ads
- Interstitial navigation count persists across app restarts, with a 10-minute display cooldown and startup protection
- Banner and native-ad requests use caching, request limits and retry delays
- App-open ads skip the first installation launch and use opening-count and cooldown eligibility
- Theme restoration, calendar state, tablet system-bar insets and widget previews improved

---

## [Current project] — 2.9

- Flexible reminders, notification actions, habits, calendar and history
- Daily Habits and Quick Add home screen widgets
- Local reminder and habit data storage
- Optional Premium and Supporter purchases managed by Google Play and RevenueCat

The current project version is 2.9. The installed Google Play release may differ.
