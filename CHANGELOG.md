## [Initial Public Release] — 2026

### Added

- Flexible one-time and repeating reminders
- Per-reminder sound, emoji, priority and vibration
- Notification snooze and completion actions
- Count, yes/no and duration habits
- Calendar, search, trigger history and summaries
- Habit and Smart Add widgets
- Premium Monthly and Lifetime options
- Local-only reminder and habit storage
- No ads, tracking or analytics

### Documentation

- Added README, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Support and Security documentation.
- Added standardized GitHub issue and pull-request templates.

For the current production release, visit [SmartRemind on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.smartreminddc.app).
